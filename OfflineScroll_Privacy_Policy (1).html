OfflineScroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · Android application: dev.offlinescroll.app

OfflineScroll is a local video player. All core browsing, playback, search, and library features work directly on your Android device without an account, server, network connection, or companion application. This policy describes the app's data practices.

Who maintains OfflineScroll

The OfflineScroll project is maintained through the GitHub account demirsrmli. The public project and privacy inquiry channel are linked below.

Videos and information accessed on your device

OfflineScroll accesses only videos you permit. It uses local video content, filenames, duration, folder information, and source references to browse and play your library. It does not modify, move, delete, or transcode your original videos, and does not copy your video library into app storage.

Likes, favorites, hidden-video flags, settings, selected-source references, and optional feed order and playback positions are stored in the app's private storage. A private metadata index of up to 16 MiB may contain filenames and basic video information to reopen the library without repeating a full scan. It does not contain copies of videos. A previously broader cached library is not reused when access becomes partial or is revoked.

Thumbnails use a bounded memory cache. Video frames are not permanently saved as thumbnails. Likes and repost-style saves only update local records; they do not publish or send videos to anyone.

No collection, uploads, or advertising

OfflineScroll does not send video content, filenames, watch history, settings, or identifiers off your device. It has no internet or network-state permission, accounts, advertising, analytics, telemetry, remote databases, cloud synchronization, or online crash-reporting service. No third-party advertising or analytics SDK collects data through the app.

Permissions and source selection

You may allow access to your local video library, permit selected videos on supported Android versions, or select individual local documents. Permission can be denied, changed, or revoked in Android settings. The system document picker may show cloud providers; OfflineScroll requests local-only sources and rejects sources it cannot verify as local, including cloud and virtual documents. It does not request access to unrelated images or permission to write to your original videos.

Storage, security, and backups

App records are stored in Android's app-private storage and protected by Android's application sandbox. Video access remains subject to Android permissions and file-access grants. Operating-system backup is disabled, and backup and extraction rules exclude app records from supported backup and device-transfer paths. Device-vendor behavior outside those Android rules is outside the app's control. OfflineScroll does not operate a server that stores your media or local records.

Retention and deletion

Persistent settings and library records may remain on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app. They have no automatic time-based expiry. Temporary thumbnails remain only in the memory cache and can be evicted as needed.

In Settings, choose Clear local app data to remove OfflineScroll settings, likes, favorites, hidden-video flags, local history, metadata caches, source references, and persisted file-access grants. Your original videos are not deleted. On Android 13 and later, runtime media permissions are also scheduled for system self-revocation. On earlier versions, revoke operating-system media permission through Android settings. You can also clear the app's storage or uninstall it through Android settings to remove its private records.

There is no OfflineScroll account or server-side user record to delete. The maintainer cannot remotely access or erase data held only on your device.

Optional external links

The app's Source code action opens https://github.com/demirsrmli/offlinescroll in another application only after an explicit tap. No video information, device identifiers, or tracking parameters are appended. OfflineScroll does not embed GitHub or automatically fetch repository content. Copying the link works offline. Browsing and playback do not depend on this action.

When you visit this policy on GitHub Pages, follow a GitHub link, or submit an inquiry on GitHub, GitHub and your browser handle that interaction under their own policies, including GitHub's Privacy Statement. Website hosting and external inquiries are separate from the app's local operation.

Privacy questions

For general privacy questions, open an issue in the OfflineScroll repository with the title “Privacy question”. A GitHub account is required to submit an issue; no GitHub account is required to use OfflineScroll.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post personal videos, filenames, media URIs, passwords, keys, contact details, or other sensitive information. Describe your question without personal data. This public channel is for general inquiries, not confidential submissions. For a security vulnerability, follow SECURITY.md in the repository and use private vulnerability reporting if enabled.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date. The policy and the app's Google Play Data safety disclosures must reflect the released app's actual behavior.
